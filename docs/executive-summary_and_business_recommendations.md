**RECOMMENDATIONS REPORT**

**NYC Taxi Jan–Jun 2025 (Sample: 300K Trips)**

**Executive Summary**

An analysis of the NYC Taxi Dataset (Jan–Jun 2025, 300K trip sample) indicates strong revenue generation totaling **$6.24M** across 300,000 completed trips. Key operational metrics highlight an average fare amount of **$13.45**, an overall revenue per trip of **$21.05** ($20.80/mile), and an average tip rate of **16.05%** ($2.16 per trip). 

These are the core principles of this multi-dashboard analysis:

**High Geographic Concentration:** Manhattan accounts for **95.11%** of total trip volume. Demand peaks sharply between **4:00 PM and 8:00 PM** across major hubs including *Midtown Center*, *Upper East Side (South & North)*, and *Penn Station/Madison Sq West*. 

**Trip Distance & Yield Dynamics:** Operations are heavily dominated by short-haul trips (overall average of **1.89 miles**). However, outer-borough rides average **3.05 to 3.51 miles** and command higher average fares (**$18.31**). Airport trips remain significantly underutilized at **0.65%** share.

**Payment Share & Congestion Impact:** Credit Cards remain the dominant payment method (**69.61% share / $4.54M**), followed by Flex Fare trips (**$1.06M**) and Cash (**$0.56M**). Congestion pricing heavily influences cost structures, with **74.69%** of trips exposed to CBD fees, generating **$589.14K** in total congestion tax and **$168.06K** in CBD fees.  

**Tipping Variations:** Tipping percentages drop significantly during early morning hours (**3:00 AM – 6:00 AM**, falling below **12%**) and peak during late afternoon/evening rush hours (**4:00 PM – 7:00 PM**, exceeding **17.5%**). 

**Business Recommendations**

Recommendation 1**:** Dynamic Fleet Positioning for Peak Demand.

Observation: Demand is heavily concentrated in Manhattan core zones (*Midtown Center*, *Upper East Side*) during the **5:00 PM – 8:00 PM** window.    
Business Risk: Insufficient vehicle availability during evening rush hours leads to higher passenger wait times, lost market share to rideshare competitors, and uncaptured surge revenue.

Action**:** Implement dynamic dispatch prompts and driver heatmaps to reallocate vehicles into top corridors (e.g., *Upper East Side South —----\> Upper East Side North*) 30 minutes prior to the 5:00 PM peak. 

Recommendation 2: Outer-Borough Incentive Program.

Observation: Outer-borough trips yield higher average fares (**$18.31**) and cover longer distances (**3.05–3.51 mi**) compared to Manhattan runs (**1.82 mi**).

Business Risk: Drivers frequently reject outer-borough drop-offs due to fear of empty, uncompensated return trips back, leaving non-Manhattan demand underserved.  

Action: Establish a return-corridor priority dispatch queue and temporary fuel/fare bonuses for drivers accepting drop-offs in outer-borough regions to guarantee profitable return legs. 

Recommendation 3: Airport Service Penetration Campaign.

Observation: Airport trips account for a minimal **0.65%** of overall trip volume despite commanding high per-trip revenue. 

Business Risk: Over-reliance on short inner-city trips leaves an opportunity to get into high-margin, long-distance revenue for competing app-based rideshare fleets and airport shuttles. 

Action: Partner with hotel groups and transit hubs to launch flat-rate airport promotional fares and establish virtual staging queues near JFK/LGA to capture high-yield airport runs.  

Recommendation 4: Smart Tipping & Off-Peak Tip Optimization.

Observation: Credit cards drive **69.61%** ($4.54M) of total revenue, but tipping percentage drops sharply during early morning hours (**3:00 AM – 6:00 AM**, dipping below **12%**).

Business Risk: Low off-peak tip rates reduce total driver earnings during early-morning and late-night shifts, leading to driver dissatisfaction, reduced fleet coverage, and higher driver turnover. 

Action: Update in-taxi card reader user interfaces to default to auto-suggested percentage prompts (e.g., 18%, 20%, 25%) or minimum fixed-dollar options on late-night to early-morning and low-fare trips. 

Recommendation 5: CBD Congestion Fee & Route Optimization.

Observation: **74.69%** of all trips are exposed to CBD congestion fees, generating **$589.14K** in congestion tax and **$168.06K** in CBD surcharges. 

Business Risk: Surcharges increase total trip costs for passengers, potentially dampening short-trip passenger demands within Central Business Districts.

Action: Integrate real-time route optimization into driver navigation to bypass CBD perimeter boundaries when practical for non-CBD destinations, and pilot pooled ride options within CBD zones to split fee burdens across riders.
